## What PMs will notice

### Dashboard (Timesheet ⏱ → Show Pending)
- **New last column: Notes 備註.** Staff notes (e.g. "2h OT") now show where you approve.
- **Possible duplicates are red,** and the Show Pending alert says how many. Check them before approving.
- **Project No. is kept exactly as typed.** `0123` stays `0123`, and `2026-09` no longer turns into a date.
- **Sorted the sheet after Show Pending?** Approve/Reject now checks the person, date, venue, rate and that the row is still Pending. If anything moved, it skips the row and tells you to run Show Pending again. It can no longer flip an already-Paid job back to Approved.

### FPS list (Staff_Directory, after Refresh Payroll)
- **Column D is now "TO PAY 待支付":** what is still owed. Pay that amount for everyone. For a **PARTIAL** person (some jobs already paid), it shows only the unpaid part.
- **⚠️ CHECK row N** (red) means a job has no usable Final Rate, usually because text was typed into OT column L. That job is **left out** of TO PAY instead of being paid as $0. Fix the row and refresh again. The refresh alert lists those rows.
- At the bottom: **TO PAY TOTAL** (what to pay now) and **GRAND TOTAL (incl. paid)**.

### Mark Approved as Paid
- *(Round 2 change.)* It asks **which payment list** accounting paid: every Refresh Payroll makes its own list, shown newest first with its HK$ total and job count. Only that list's jobs are marked Paid. A job approved later stays Approved, even if a later refresh listed it.
- The very first time after deploying, it asks you to run Refresh Payroll first.

### Notion sync
- Crew members with many jobs (over 25 links) no longer lose old links. Long Work Logs (over 2000 characters) no longer fail on every sync, and are no longer cut off.
- `+852 …` and `9123-4567` phone formats in Notion are matched, so no new duplicate Crew pages are created.
- On a very big sync, it may say **"stopped early (time limit)"**. Use **Timesheet ⏱ → 🔄 Sync to Notion** to finish; nothing gets written twice. *(Round 2 change.)*
- At 100 job links (Notion's limit), the row is **not** synced. Sync_Log shows an ERROR and column P gets a note. Link it by hand, then type a date in P. *(Round 2 change.)*

### Staff page
- If the reply is lost, the page checks the server and says **"Saved"** only if a new matching record really appeared. That means same date, venue, rate, start **and** end, and not Rejected. Before, a different shift on the same day, or a Rejected record, could make it say "Saved" wrongly.
- If only some jobs in a batch saved, it lists the saved ones and leaves only the unsaved ones on the form to resend.
- A resend never creates a duplicate row once the new backend is live (each job carries a hidden id).
- If the access code is changed, phones go back to the code screen and keep what was typed. Before, they were stuck with "Invalid access code".
- Buttons are at least 48 px tall.

### Security
- The web-app link now shows plain text only. Before, a visitor could use that page to run the script's internal functions without the access code, including reading or changing the Notion HR data.
- Anything staff type that starts with `=` `+` `-` `@` is stored as plain text, never as a formula.

## Round 2: independent review fixes

An independent reviewer read PR #2 and found one major and five minor defects. All six are fixed on the same branch, each with a test of the reviewer's exact scenario.

| # | What the reviewer found | What changed | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (major) | Every Refresh Payroll **added** to one saved payment list. Refresh (A $800) → accounting pays A → B ($1000) approved → refresh again → Mark as Paid marked **A and B** Paid, so B was never paid. The Notion time-out message even told PMs to refresh again. | Each Refresh Payroll saves its **own** payment list (time, months, HK$ total, jobs). **Mark Approved as Paid** shows the unpaid lists newest first, with HK$ total and job count, and marks **only the one you pick** (nothing is pre-selected, so you can't take the newest by habit). The confirm box shows that list's total. Paid lists disappear and other lists drop jobs that are now Paid. Unpaid lists expire after 45 days. New menu item **🔄 Sync to Notion** finishes a timed-out sync without rebuilding payroll, and the time-out message now points to it. | R01–R09 |
| 2 | Approve/Reject didn't compare start/end times, so two same-day shifts at the same venue and rate could swap Project No./Role/PIC after a sort. | The guard also compares the shift times (Dashboard "Hours" vs columns G–H). | R12, R13 |
| 3 | If writing the Final Rate formula failed, the row was saved but reported "not saved", so a retry duplicated it. | The row is recorded as saved (and its retry id remembered) right after it is written, before the formula. Payroll's F + L fallback covers the missing formula. | R14 |
| 4 | Stale entries stayed on the payment list forever (job marked Paid by hand, or edited), so a later identical job could be marked Paid without being listed. | Lists expire (#1). Each job is matched by phone, date, venue, amount **and submission time**, so a later identical job never matches. Each refresh and mark drops entries that are no longer Approved exactly as listed. | R10, R11 |
| 5 | Work dates were read in Hong Kong time, not the Sheet's own time zone. In a sheet set to a zone ahead of HK, 1 Sep read as 31 Aug. | Work dates are read in the Sheet's own time zone everywhere (status, duplicate check, Dashboard guard, payroll, Job_Summary, Notion). "Now" timestamps stay in HK time. | R15 (Auckland and New York), R16 |
| 6 | At Notion's 100-link limit, the row was stamped Synced anyway, so the missing link was easy to miss. | At the limit nothing is written for that row, and it stays **unsynced**. Sync_Log shows an ERROR, column P gets a note, and the end-of-sync alert counts it. Old links are never deleted (that would lose work history). Once a link slot is free, the next sync goes through and clears the note. | N14 (rewritten) |

- New menu item **Timesheet ⏱ → 🔄 Sync to Notion**: use it to finish a sync that stopped at the time limit, or to sync without rebuilding payroll.
- A row that can't be linked in Notion (100-link limit) has a note in column P and stays unsynced until it is sorted out.

## Round 3: re-review of the payment lists

A re-review of round 2 found two minor issues in the payment lists. Both are fixed.

| # | What the re-review found | What changed | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Only the newest 10 lists were kept, and every refresh saved a list, even an identical one. After 10 more refreshes, the older $800 list holding only job A was gone. A could then only be marked Paid through a bigger list that included unpaid jobs. | The count cap is removed. A list leaves only when it is **fully paid** or after **45 days**. A refresh with exactly the same jobs as the newest unpaid list doesn't add another list. Each job key is stored once, with lists pointing to it, so many lists stay far below Google's 500 KB storage limit (30 lists of up to 300 jobs use under 100 KB). | T01–T05 |
| 2 | Picker labels were to the minute, so two lists made in the same minute looked alike. | Lists are numbered **#1 (newest), #2, …** and show the time to the second, plus HK$ total and job count. The confirm box repeats the number. | T06 |
